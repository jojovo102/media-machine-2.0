## 1. Brief
- Italian, Catholic, for @volpinveritas (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube). Scripts written by Josef during the week (one a
  day, 12:00) and checked by the priest who helps him; recorded on Saturday 14:30 to 18:00.
## 2. Feel
- Him, close, on his phone, speaking with conviction; the faith and the history carry it, not effects.
## 3. Look (as he edits it)
- Phone selfie, vertical 1080x1920, full frame: no zooms, no punch-ins, no captions burned in.
- Cuts every 1.5 to 3 seconds out of one long take (30 to 45 cuts in 85 to 120 s).
- B-roll (not built yet): artwork and photos (Lepanto, Pio V, the Madonna of the Rosary) as plain cards over the
  lower half of the frame, 1 to 3 seconds each; a screen recording of volpinveritas.com near the end.
## 4. Rhythm
- Speed 1.05x (his projects: 1.01 to 1.05x). Pauses between words over 0.1 s cut.
- A cut falls where his voice starts and stops, with no air: on his Lepanto project 0.01 s of quiet at a cut (2026-10-21).
## 5. Sound
- His voice first, as his mic recorded it: his CapCut projects only raise its volume (+10 dB), never clean it. The
  machine does the same: one fixed gain and a slow peak limiter, no noise reduction, no compressor (2026-10-21).
  Copyright-free music under it, low and steady (his: "Classical Cello Experience", Scott Buckley's "Legionnaire").
  Master -14.5 LUFS, where his published reels sit (-14.1 to -14.5).
## 5b. Captions and cards (2026-10-21, on his request for "Contro l'AI nei video")
- Captions never go on by themselves: he sees them on the reel first (a preview copy), and only after his OK does the
  batch get them (style.json per batch). Josef, 2026-10-21: "before we do the captions for each video, I want to see them first".
- Captions when he has OK'd them: EB Garamond 600, cream #F1E9DC, the word being said in the
  logo's red #B01018 (his choice over gold, 2026-10-21), three words at a time, just under his chin (y 0.52), above the cards.
- Cards over the lower half (y 0.58 to 0.86, 860 px wide): public-domain paintings (never AI-generated art: the page
  speaks against AI-made faith content), and cards drawn in the site's look (near-black card, gold rule, Cinzel titles,
  Garamond text). 1.5 to 6 s each, popping in, never over his face.
## 6. Words
- Only what he said. The script is the reference for what to keep, never something to put on screen by itself.
